# EFF word list attribution

`eff_large_wordlist.txt` is the unmodified EFF long English word list published by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) in 2016. It contains 7,776 dice-code and word pairs. The generator capitalizes selected words at runtime; the vendored file retains its original bytes.

Source: [EFF long word list](https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt).

Background: [EFF's New Wordlists for Random Passphrases](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases).
